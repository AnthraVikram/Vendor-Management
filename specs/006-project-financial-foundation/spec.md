## Context

The firm already records **client advances** on a project (Feature 003): an Administrator or Accountant records the amount, date received, method, reference and note; a mistake is corrected by a full reversal with a reason; nothing is edited or deleted; the project shows the net amount received; the dashboard shows two money tiles; every entry is in history. That works and stays.

What is missing is everything around it: the quotation the client was given and its revisions, which revision the client approved, the client's purchase order, payments received after the advance, how much the client still owes, and one place to see all of it in order. Today these live in Excel, e-mail and paper.

This feature turns the project's client advances into a **client finance** area that holds:

| Term | Meaning in this feature | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Client quotation (with revisions) | An amount the firm formally quotes to the client; each change of amount is a new numbered revision | Fin. ref. §4.1, §6.1 to 6.3 |
| Approved client amount | The amount of the exact quotation revision the client approved (Q3) | Brief; fin. ref. §4.1 "Agreed Client Amount" |
| Client purchase order (PO) | The client's own purchase order issued to the firm for the project | Brief (new term; not in the financial reference) |
| Client receipt | Money received from the client: a **client advance** (Feature 003) or a **subsequent payment** | Brief; fin. ref. §6.4 |
| Outstanding client balance | What the client still owes the firm on the project, the approved client amount minus all verified, unreversed receipts (FR-034) | Brief; fin. ref. §6.4 "Outstanding client receivables" |
| Client financial history | Every client financial record and correction on the project, in date order | Brief |

A client quotation is the firm's quotation **to its client**. A client PO is the **client's** order to the firm. Neither is a vendor quotation (PRD FR-10), a task price or costing sheet (PRD FR-18), or a vendor purchase order (financial reference §19). Client receipts are never vendor payments (PRD section 8; Feature 003 FR-022).

Role names: the brief's "Office Staff" is the existing **Office Team** role (Feature 001 FR-004).

### Redefinition 2026-11-17

- Feature 006 is redefined as **Client Financial Management**: client POs; client quotations and revisions; approved client amounts; client advances and subsequent payment receipts; outstanding client balances; financial documents and transaction history; integration with Feature 003 client advances; audit trails and controlled corrections.
- Permissions: Administrator, Accountant and Office Team view financial information. Office Team enters and submits financial data. Administrator and Accountant review, approve and manage verified financial records. Office Team cannot approve or modify approved financial records.

- Not restated in the redefinition and therefore open: client budget and internal costing estimate (confirmed 2026-11-12) — OQ-1. (Decided later the same day: removed from this feature; see Session 2026-11-17.)
- OQ-3: How does a client PO relate to the quotation? → A: Option A. A client PO must reference an approved client quotation, and its amount must exactly match the approved quotation amount. If the client amount changes, the quotation must be revised and approved again before a matching PO can be recorded. The approved quotation remains the authoritative source for the agreed (approved) client amount. (FR-029, FR-032)
- OQ-8: Can an Approved quotation be revised? → A: An Approved quotation revision must never be edited, reopened or have its approval overwritten. A new revision may be created from an Approved quotation; it starts as Draft and must follow the complete existing workflow, Draft → Submitted → Under review → Approved or Rejected. The previously approved revision and its approval record remain permanently preserved. The new revision becomes the current approved client amount only when it is approved; until then the previous approved revision remains authoritative. Any new purchase order must reference an approved revision and match its approved amount exactly. (FR-023, FR-025a, FR-027, FR-028, FR-032; resolves Conflict C6)
- OQ-32: How do revisions and statuses relate? → A: Each quotation revision has its own revision number and its own status; revision history and status are separate concepts. Creating a new revision after an Approved revision starts that new revision in Draft, without changing the previous approved revision or its approval. A later revision can be withdrawn while it is in Draft or Submitted, under the Q2 withdrawal rules. Once a revision is Under review, Approved or Rejected it cannot be withdrawn; Approved and Rejected remain terminal for that revision. (FR-023, FR-025, FR-025a. The "approval round" concept drafted earlier on 2026-11-17 is withdrawn: per-revision statuses express the same behaviour.)
- Sequential quotation revisions (Option A): How may revisions follow one another? → A: Revisions follow a strictly sequential workflow. Only one revision per quotation may be in an active status (Draft, Submitted or Under review) at a time. A new revision cannot be created while an existing revision is Draft, Submitted or Under review; it can be created only after the previous revision reaches a final status (Approved, Rejected or Withdrawn). Every new revision starts in Draft. Each revision has its own status and independent history. Creating a new revision never modifies the status, amount, approval or history of an earlier revision. An Approved revision and its approval record are immutable. The latest successfully approved revision is authoritative for the client's agreed amount; a rejected or withdrawn revision cannot replace it; while a newer revision is pending, the previously approved revision remains authoritative; approving a newer revision makes its amount authoritative without modifying earlier approval records. Existing purchase orders are not automatically changed, cancelled or replaced when a newer revision is approved. (FR-022, FR-023, FR-023a, FR-025a, FR-026, FR-028; resolves OQ-10, OQ-11, OQ-33, OQ-34.)
- OQ-9: May a Draft revision be edited in place? → A: Option A. Draft revisions cannot be edited in place. To correct a Draft, it is withdrawn and a new revision created. The append-only financial history is preserved; there are no draft versions and no editing exception to the Constitution. (FR-005)
- OQ-13 (who may withdraw): → A: Option B. Office Team members may withdraw Draft revisions only. Submitted revisions can be withdrawn only by an Administrator or Accountant. Approved, Rejected and Under review revisions cannot be withdrawn. The existing status-transition and audit-history rules are unchanged. Whether a reason is mandatory for Rejected and Withdrawn stays open. (Refined later the same day by follow-up decisions 2 and 3 below: Office Team withdraws only Drafts they created; a reason is mandatory.) (FR-001, FR-025)
- OQ-35(f): May a PO be recorded against the authoritative approval while a newer revision is pending? → A: Option C. Yes, even while a newer revision is Draft, Submitted or Under review. A warning identifies the pending revision and its amount, and explicit confirmation is required before the PO is recorded. The PO must still reference the authoritative approved revision and match its amount exactly. A later approval must not automatically modify the existing PO. OQ-35(a) to (e) stay open (decided later the same day; see Session 2026-11-17). (FR-032a)

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

"Reviewer" below means an Administrator or an Accountant. "Every role" means Administrator, Accountant and Office Team.

### User Story 1 - Keep recording client advances exactly as today (Priority: P1)

An Administrator or Accountant records and reverses client advances exactly as Feature 003 allows, and every advance already in the system, including reversed ones, stays as it is, with the same amounts, dates, authors, reversals, net amount, dashboard tiles and history.

**Why this priority**: Preservation is the first requirement of the redefinition; nothing else may break it.

Only one revision is ever in progress at a time. Earlier revisions are never overwritten, and the newest revision is always marked.

**Why this priority**: The approved client amount, the PO and the outstanding balance all start from a quotation.

**Independent Test**: As Office Team, create a quotation and submit revision 1; as an Accountant, mark it Under review and reject it; as Office Team, add revision 2 with a reason and submit it: revisions 1 and 2 are listed with amount, date, reason, author, time and their own statuses (Rejected, Submitted); revision 2 is the newest; adding revision 3 now is refused; no request can change or delete revision 1.

23. **Given** an archived project with a Submitted PO and a Verified PO, **When** anyone tries to enter a new PO or verify the Submitted PO, **Then** it is refused; **When** a reviewer rejects the Submitted PO or cancels the Verified PO with a reason, **Then** the correction is saved (FR-010; OQ-26).
24. **Given** a Submitted PO with a wrong PO number entered by an Office Team member, **When** that member withdraws it with a reason, **Then** it becomes Withdrawn and is no longer current, and the correct PO can then be entered against the same approved revision; the withdrawn PO cannot be resubmitted (FR-030, FR-031; OQ-17).
25. **Given** a Submitted PO with no document of type Purchase Order attached, **When** a reviewer tries to verify it, **Then** it is refused until a PO document is attached; **When** an Administrator or Accountant enters a PO, which is verified on entry, without a PO document, **Then** it is refused (FR-030; OQ-25).
26. **Given** a Cancelled PO, **When** an Administrator or Accountant attaches a document, **Then** it is attached and the PO stays Cancelled; **When** an Office Team member tries to attach one, or anyone tries to remove an attachment, **Then** it is refused (FR-042b, FR-042d; OQ-40).
27. **Given** a PO entered without a PO number, PO date, amount or approved quotation reference, **When** it is saved, **Then** it is refused with a message naming each missing field (FR-029; OQ-6).
28. **Given** the same PO number recorded for a client named "Mehta Interiors Pvt Ltd" or "Mehta Int.", **When** it is saved for "Mehta Interiors", **Then** no warning is shown, because the names differ after normalisation (FR-029a; OQ-41).

### User Story 6 - See the outstanding client balance and the project's client financial history (Priority: P2)

Any staff member opens a project and sees its approved client amount, verified POs, verified receipts and the outstanding client balance, each with what it is made of, and a single date-ordered history of every client financial record, verification, rejection and reversal.

**Why this priority**: It answers "how much does the client still owe?" and makes the records usable; it needs Stories 1 to 5.

**Independent Test**: With a seeded project holding an approval, a verified PO, a verified advance, a verified subsequent payment, a submitted (unverified) payment and a reversed advance, open the project as each role: the balance equals the approved client amount minus the verified, unreversed advance and subsequent payment (FR-034) exactly to the paisa, lists the records it uses, and excludes the unverified and reversed ones; the history lists every event in date order.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a project with the records above, **When** any role opens it, **Then** the outstanding client balance is shown with its basis and the receipts it counts, computed as the authoritative approved client amount minus all verified, unreversed receipts, advances and subsequent payments alike (FR-034).
2. **Given** a project with no approved client amount, **When** it is shown, **Then** the balance reads "Not available: no approved amount" rather than a number.
3. **Given** verified, unreversed receipts exceeding the approved client amount, **When** the balance is shown, **Then** it reads "₹0.00 outstanding" with a separate "Overpaid by ₹X (incl. GST)", and never a negative outstanding amount (FR-036; OQ-22).
4. **Given** the project's client financial history, **When** it is opened, **Then** every quotation, revision, status change, approval, PO, receipt, verification, rejection and reversal appears once, in date order, with its amount where it has one, person and time (P-5, accepted).
5. **Given** a submitted, rejected or reversed receipt, **When** the balance is shown, **Then** it is not counted, and the history shows it with its state.
6. **Given** a project with an approved quotation but no purchase order, **When** it is opened, **Then** the outstanding balance is shown, because no purchase order is needed (FR-034).
7. **Given** a project with no approved quotation, **When** a receipt is saved or verified, **Then** no overpayment warning is shown, and the balance reads "Not available" (FR-035, FR-036; OQ-22).

### Edge Cases

- **Existing reversed advance**: stays reversed, never counted, never re-verified (FR-012, FR-013).
- **Receipt entered on a project with no quotation**: allowed, as advances are today; the balance shows "Not available" until a quotation revision is approved (FR-034, FR-035).
- **Receipt or PO submitted, then the project archived before verification**: it cannot be verified while the project is archived; a reviewer may reject it with a reason, or verify it after the project is restored (FR-010; OQ-26).
- **Wrong entry still awaiting verification**: its Office Team creator withdraws it with a reason and enters a correct one; a Withdrawn or Rejected entry is never reopened (FR-018, FR-030; OQ-17).
- **Quotation revision active when the project is archived**: it stays in its status (Draft, Submitted or Under review) and can be neither withdrawn, reviewed nor approved until the project is restored (FR-010; OQ-26).
- **Office Team member enters, then is revoked**: their submission stays, attributed to them (Feature 001 FR-006), and can still be verified or rejected.
- **Same person enters and verifies**: a reviewer's own entry is verified on entry (P-2), so no reviewer ever verifies their own receipt; only Office Team receipts wait, and any Administrator or Accountant may verify or reject them (OQ-16).
- **Receipt larger than the outstanding balance**: allowed after an explicit, recorded confirmation of the overpayment warning; the balance then shows ₹0.00 outstanding and "Overpaid by ₹X (incl. GST)" (FR-036; OQ-22).
- **Overpayment arising without a new receipt** (for example a lower revision approved later): the balance shows ₹0.00 outstanding and "Overpaid by ₹X (incl. GST)"; no warning is shown, because no receipt is being saved or verified (FR-036).
- **Approved amount must change**: a new revision is created after the Approved revision, starts in Draft and goes through its own statuses (OQ-8, OQ-32); the earlier approval stays authoritative until the new one is approved; a PO for the new amount can be recorded only after that approval (OQ-3).
- **New revision rejected or withdrawn**: the earlier approved revision stays authoritative (FR-028); a further revision may then be created, starting in Draft (FR-023a).
- **Amount in a Draft revision needs correcting**: a Draft is never edited (OQ-9) and no new revision can be added while it is Draft (FR-023a); it is withdrawn with a reason — by its creator if an Office Team member, or by any Administrator or Accountant — and a new revision created (OQ-13). The withdrawn Draft stays in the history with its original amount.
- **Office Team member's Draft whose creator is unavailable** (absent or revoked): only an Administrator or Accountant can withdraw it (OQ-13); until then no new revision can be created (FR-023a).
- **Document attached to a Draft that is later withdrawn**: the attachment stays listed on the withdrawn revision and in its history; Withdrawn is final, so it can no longer be removed (FR-042d), only superseded by an Administrator or Accountant (FR-042e).
- **Office Team needs a Submitted revision withdrawn**: only an Administrator or Accountant can do it (OQ-13); until then no new revision can be created (FR-023a).
- **Two people create the next revision at once**: one succeeds; the other is refused because a revision is now active (FR-007, FR-023a).
- **PO matched to an earlier approved revision when a later revision is approved**: it stays as recorded, labelled "Matches superseded approval (revision n)"; nothing changes automatically; a reviewer cancels it when the client replaces it (FR-031, FR-032b).
- **PO recorded while a newer revision is pending**: allowed against the authoritative approval after a warning naming the pending revision and its amount and an explicit confirmation (FR-032a).
- **PO entered before the quotation is approved**: refused (FR-032); it is entered once the approval exists.
- **Same client written differently on two projects** (for example "Mehta Interiors" and "Mehta Interiors Pvt Ltd"): their POs are not compared, so a duplicate PO number is not flagged; the warning is a help, not a guarantee (FR-029a; OQ-41).
- **Several quotations on one project**: refused; additional work or a changed amount is a new revision of the one quotation (FR-022; OQ-7). **Several POs on one project**: at most one current (Submitted or Verified) PO per approved revision; a cancelled or rejected PO does not count, so a replacement may follow (FR-031; OQ-4). Several POs on a project can therefore exist only across cancellations, rejections or different approved revisions (OQ-35).
- **Document archived after being attached**: stays attached, marked archived, openable (Feature 003 FR-015). Archiving the document is not removing the attachment link; any role may archive it, as today (FR-042c).
- **Evidence attached during review**: allowed for every role; its actual attachment time is kept, so it is clear the document was added while the record was Under review (FR-042b).
- **Corrected document**: uploaded as a new project document and attached, optionally with a "supersedes" reference that requires a reason; the original document is never overwritten or replaced, and its attachment stays visible, labelled superseded (FR-042c, FR-042e). Once superseded, the original attachment can no longer be removed, even on an active record (FR-042d; OQ-39).
- **Correcting a correction**: the new attachment is current and not superseded, so a later attachment may supersede it in turn; each attachment can be superseded only once (FR-042e). A wrong correction is never removed; it is itself superseded, so the chain stays complete (OQ-39).
- **Document re-attached after removal**: allowed as a new attachment; the removed link stays in the Removed Attachments list (FR-042f).
- **Attacher unavailable** (absent or revoked): their attachments on an active record can be removed only by an Administrator or Accountant, and only if not part of a supersede chain (FR-042d; OQ-39).
- **One document on several records**: removing it from one record leaves its other links unchanged (FR-042f).
- **Two reviewers verify the same receipt at once**: one succeeds; the other is told it is already verified.
- **Feature 005 "Budget & Costing" section**: stays text-only and unrelated (FR-050).

#### Integrity (all stories)

- **FR-004**: Every amount MUST be Indian rupees with at most two decimal places, stored and compared exactly, greater than zero, and no more than ₹9,999,999,999,999.99 (Constitution III; Feature 003 FR-018). Zero and negative amounts MUST be refused for every new financial amount (OQ-24).
- **FR-005**: Client financial records MUST be append-only: no receipt, reversal, quotation revision, status change, approval, PO, verification or rejection MUST be edited or deleted by anyone through any route (Constitution III). Verification, rejection, approval and reversal MUST each be a new entry linked to the record, never a change to it (P-2). This includes revisions in Draft: a Draft MUST NOT be edited in place, and is corrected by withdrawing it and creating a new revision (OQ-9; FR-023a). No draft versions and no editing exception to the Constitution exist. Attaching a document to a Draft is not an edit (FR-042a).
- **FR-006**: Each entry MUST record who made it and when, from the signed-in session.
- **FR-007**: An action that depends on current state (a revision, a status change, an approval, a verification, a rejection, a reversal) MUST be refused if that state changed since the person loaded it.
- **FR-008**: An action that writes more than one record (for example an approval and its status change) MUST save all of them or none.
- **FR-009**: Every client financial amount — quotation revision, approved client amount, PO, receipt and outstanding balance — MUST be the GST-inclusive total payable by the client (OQ-23). The system MUST NOT calculate, split or store GST, and MUST NOT offer any tax field; any base-and-GST breakdown stays in the attached documents (FR-041). Tax calculation and filing stay out of scope (Constitution Product Boundaries).
- **FR-010**: Archiving or restoring a project MUST NOT change, hide or delete any client financial record. While a project is archived, its client financial records MUST be read-only except for these corrections, which MUST stay allowed to the roles FR-001 gives them: reversing a verified receipt (Feature 003 FR-020), rejecting a Submitted receipt or PO with a reason, and cancelling a Verified PO with a reason (FR-031). Every other action MUST be refused: any new receipt (Feature 003 FR-018), PO, quotation or revision; any verification or approval; any other status change, including submitting, withdrawing and marking Under review; and attaching or removing a document (OQ-26). Restoring the project makes these actions available again.
- **FR-011**: Nothing in this feature MUST create, alter or remove a vendor payment, vendor record or vendor-project assignment; client receipts MUST NOT be linked to a vendor (Feature 003 FR-022).

#### Existing client advances (US1; confirmed preservation)

No migration MUST rewrite, re-date, re-attribute, merge or delete them.
- **FR-013**: Each existing advance MUST be presented as a client receipt of type "Advance" in the state it already has: standing advances as verified, reversed advances as reversed (P-1, accepted; OQ-18). No verification entry MUST be invented for them; the receipt shows "Recorded under Feature 003" with its original author and time.
- **FR-014**: Feature 003 rules for advances MUST continue to hold for every receipt of type Advance: amount greater than zero with two decimals; date received no later than today; methods Cash, Bank transfer, Cheque, UPI or Other; optional reference and note; refusal on an archived project; no edit or delete; full-amount reversal with a reason, at most once, allowed on an archived project, never itself reversed (Feature 003 FR-018 to FR-022).
- **FR-015**: The project's net client advances received (Feature 003 FR-021), the dashboard total and month tiles (FR-024), the dashboard drill-down list of standing advances (FR-026) and the dashboard's handling of advance history (Feature 005 FR-023) MUST give the same results as before for every record that exists today. They MUST continue to cover client advances only and MUST count only advances that are verified and not reversed: Submitted, Rejected and Withdrawn advances and every subsequent payment MUST be excluded, and their labels MUST stay unchanged (OQ-27). Because every existing advance is verified (FR-013), existing values do not change.

#### Client receipts (US2)

- **FR-016**: Any staff member MUST be able to enter a client receipt on an active project with a type, amount, date received, optional method, reference and note, and optional documents. The type MUST be one of Advance, Stage payment, Final payment or Other (OQ-15; P-3, accepted). Advance MUST keep its Feature 003 behaviour (FR-014), and only Advance feeds the dashboard money tiles and net client advances (FR-015). Every type counts towards the outstanding balance when verified and unreversed (FR-034); the type never changes any amount or calculation.
- **FR-017**: A receipt entered by Office Team MUST be saved as Submitted. A receipt entered by an Administrator or Accountant MUST be saved as verified, as Feature 003 records an advance today (P-2, accepted; OQ-16). Any Administrator or Accountant may verify or reject a Submitted receipt (FR-018).
- **FR-018**: A reviewer MUST be able to verify a Submitted receipt, or reject it with a reason. Each is a new entry with person and time. A rejected receipt MUST stay readable and MUST never count as received. The Office Team member who entered a Submitted receipt MAY withdraw it with a mandatory reason, and an Administrator or Accountant MAY withdraw any Submitted receipt with a mandatory reason; the withdrawal MUST be a new entry with the reason, person and time, checked against the recorded creator for Office Team (FR-006). A Withdrawn receipt MUST stay readable and MUST never count as received. A Rejected or Withdrawn receipt MUST NOT be returned to Submitted; a correction MUST be entered as a new receipt (OQ-17).
- **FR-019**: Only receipts that are verified and not reversed MUST count as money received anywhere in this feature.
- **FR-020**: A reviewer MUST be able to reverse a verified receipt of any type with a mandatory reason, under the Feature 003 FR-020 rules: always the full amount, at most once, as a new entry linked to the receipt, allowed on an archived project, and never itself reversed. Partial reversal MUST NOT be offered for any type (OQ-20); a wrong amount MUST be corrected by reversing the receipt in full and entering a new receipt. Client refunds are not part of this feature.
- **FR-021**: A receipt MAY be linked to a verified PO or an approved quotation of the same project (P-4, accepted). The link is optional for every receipt type; a receipt, including an advance, MAY be recorded before any PO exists (OQ-19). An approved quotation means any approved revision of the project's quotation, including one whose approval has been superseded. A linked PO MUST be Verified, and not Cancelled, when the receipt is entered. A later cancellation of the PO or a later approval MUST NOT change or invalidate an existing receipt's link (decision 2026-11-18).

#### Client quotations and revisions (US3; Q2 kept)

- **FR-022**: Each project MUST have at most one client quotation (OQ-7); a request to create a second MUST be refused, and any change or additional work MUST be a new revision of that quotation (FR-023). Any staff member MUST be able to create the client quotation on a project with a title, an amount, a quotation date no later than today (India Standard Time) and an optional note. It MUST start at revision 1 in status Draft (sequential revision rule, 2026-11-17). The quotation MUST receive a system-generated reference, and each revision a system-generated number (FR-023); neither is entered by staff. Each revision's submission date MUST be recorded automatically when it moves to Submitted. A validity date and a client acceptance date MAY be recorded and are optional (OQ-14).
- **FR-023**: Any staff member MUST be able to add a revision with an amount, a quotation date, a required reason and an optional note, subject to FR-023a. Revisions MUST be numbered 1, 2, 3 … per quotation without gaps or duplicates; the highest is the newest. Each revision MUST have its own status and its own history (FR-025; OQ-32). Every new revision MUST start in Draft. Creating a revision MUST NOT change the status, amount, approval or history of any earlier revision.
- **FR-023a**: Revisions of a quotation MUST be strictly sequential (sequential revision rule, 2026-11-17). Draft, Submitted and Under review are active statuses; Approved, Rejected and Withdrawn are final. At most one revision of a quotation MUST be in an active status at any time. A request to create a revision while any revision of that quotation is Draft, Submitted or Under review MUST be refused with a message naming that revision and its status. A new revision MAY be created once the newest revision is Approved, Rejected or Withdrawn. Two simultaneous requests to create the next revision MUST result in at most one new revision (FR-007).
- **FR-024**: Every revision MUST stay readable for the life of the record with its number, amount, date, reason, note, documents, person and time, showing the previous and new amount.
- **FR-025**: Each revision MUST have exactly one current status — Draft, Submitted, Under review, Approved, Rejected or Withdrawn — and only these changes of a revision's status MUST be allowed (Q2; OQ-32); every other change MUST be refused with a message naming the current status:

  | From | To | Who (FR-001) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Draft | Submitted | Every role |
  | Draft | Withdrawn | Reviewer: any Draft; Office Team: only a Draft they created (OQ-13) |
  | Submitted | Under review | Reviewer |
  | Submitted | Withdrawn | Reviewer only (OQ-13) |
  | Under review | Approved (only through FR-026) | Reviewer |
  | Under review | Rejected | Reviewer |

  For each revision, Approved, Rejected and Withdrawn have no change out of them, and a revision that is Under review, Approved or Rejected cannot be withdrawn (OQ-32). Each change MUST be kept with the revision it concerns, previous status, new status, optional note, person and time. A reason MUST be given for every change to Rejected or Withdrawn, by any role; a request without a reason MUST be refused; the reason MUST be recorded with the status change in the revision's audit history (OQ-13). For withdrawing a Draft, the server MUST compare the requester with the revision's recorded creator (FR-006) when the requester is an Office Team member. Under review means a reviewer has started checking a Submitted revision (OQ-12).
- **FR-025a**: The quotation MUST show every revision with its number, amount and own status, and separately its authoritative approved revision (FR-028). A revision's status MUST change only through its own FR-025 transitions; no action on one revision MUST change another revision's status or approval (OQ-32). At most one revision is active at a time (FR-023a).

#### Approved client amount (US4; Q3 kept)

- **FR-026**: Approving MUST be the Under review → Approved change of one revision by a reviewer; that revision is the revision approved. Approval MUST be refused unless a document of type Quotation is attached to that revision or is supplied with the approval (OQ-25; decision 2026-11-18). Documents supplied with the approval are attached to the approval by the reviewer, under the same project, archived-document and duplicate rules as any attachment (FR-042f); the approval, its status change, its attachment links and all their history entries MUST be saved together (FR-008). In the same save the system MUST record an approval with that revision, the approved client amount taken from that revision's amount, an optional note and documents, the person and the time. A request supplying a different amount MUST be refused. A revision MUST NOT be approved once a newer revision of the same quotation exists (FR-023a makes this impossible in normal use, because only the newest revision can be Under review); the server MUST still refuse any such request.
- **FR-027**: An Approved revision MUST NOT be edited or reopened, and its approval record MUST NOT be changed, replaced or overwritten (OQ-8). A changed amount MUST be a new revision (FR-023), starting in Draft, that reaches Approved through its own FR-025 transitions (Q3, OQ-8, OQ-32). Every earlier approved revision and its approval record MUST remain permanently readable; when a later approval is recorded, the earlier one MUST be shown as superseded by it, without any change to its record.
- **FR-028**: The quotation's authoritative approved revision MUST be its latest successfully approved revision; under FR-023a this is both its most recent approval and its highest-numbered Approved revision. The approved client amount MUST be that revision's amount, shown with the quotation and revision it came from. While a newer revision is Draft, Submitted or Under review, the previously approved revision MUST remain authoritative. A Rejected or Withdrawn revision MUST NOT replace or change the approved client amount. Approving a newer revision MUST make its amount authoritative without modifying any earlier approval record (FR-027). A quotation with no Approved revision has no approved client amount. Because a project has one quotation (OQ-7), the project's approved client amount is that quotation's authoritative approved amount.

#### Client purchase orders (US5; confirmed scope, details open)

- **FR-029**: Any staff member MUST be able to enter a client PO on an active project, referencing one approved client quotation of that project (its approval and approved revision, FR-026), with the client's PO number, PO date no later than today and amount, all required, and an optional note and documents; a Purchase order document is required before the PO is verified (FR-030) (OQ-3, OQ-6). A PO missing its number, date, amount or approved quotation reference MUST be refused; any other field is optional.
- **FR-029a**: When a PO is saved with a PO number already recorded on a PO of the same project, or of any project whose client name matches after trimming leading and trailing whitespace, collapsing repeated spaces and ignoring letter case, the person MUST be warned of a possible duplicate, naming the earlier PO and its project, and the PO MUST be saved only after they confirm; it MUST NOT be blocked automatically (P-7; OQ-41). Client names that differ in spelling, punctuation or alias are not matched, so the warning is a help, not a guarantee (Assumption A2: a client is only the client name on each project).
- **FR-030**: A PO MUST NOT be verified, and a PO entered by an Administrator or Accountant (verified on entry) MUST NOT be saved, unless a document of type Purchase Order is attached (OQ-25). A PO entered by an Office Team member MUST be saved as Submitted; any Administrator or Accountant MUST be able to verify it, or reject it with a reason, each as a new entry with person and time. A PO entered by an Administrator or Accountant MUST be saved as Verified, as receipts are (FR-017), so no reviewer verifies their own PO (OQ-5). A Submitted PO MAY be withdrawn under the same rule as a Submitted receipt (FR-018): by the Office Team member who entered it or by any Administrator or Accountant, with a mandatory reason; Withdrawn is final, and a Rejected or Withdrawn PO MUST NOT be returned to Submitted (OQ-17).
- **FR-031**: A PO's number, date, amount and referenced approval MUST NOT change once saved. At most one current PO — one that is Submitted or Verified — MAY reference the same approved revision; entering another while one is current MUST be refused (OQ-4). An Administrator or Accountant MAY cancel a Verified PO by giving a mandatory reason; the cancellation MUST be a new, auditable entry with the reason, person and time, recorded in the PO's history, and the PO MUST stay readable with all its details, shown as Cancelled. A Cancelled, Rejected or Withdrawn PO is not current, so a replacement PO MAY then reference the same approved revision under FR-032. Office Team MUST NOT cancel a PO; a Submitted PO is rejected, not cancelled (FR-030).
- **FR-032**: A client PO MUST reference an approved revision (an approval record, FR-026) of a quotation on the same project, and its amount MUST exactly equal that approval's approved client amount, to the paisa (OQ-3, OQ-8). A PO with no approval to reference, referencing a revision that is not approved or is on another project, or with any other amount MUST be refused with a message giving the reason and, for an amount mismatch, the approved amount. The approved quotation MUST remain the authoritative source of the approved client amount; a PO MUST NOT set, change or replace it. When the client amount changes, a matching PO MUST be recorded only after the new revision is approved (FR-027). Approving a newer revision MUST NOT automatically change, cancel, replace or invalidate any existing PO. A new PO MUST reference only the authoritative approval (FR-028); POs matched to an earlier approval follow FR-032b. At most one current PO may reference the same approval (FR-031). Recording a later approval MUST NOT change any existing PO record.
- **FR-032a**: A PO MAY be recorded against the authoritative approved revision (FR-028) while a newer revision of that quotation is Draft, Submitted or Under review (OQ-35(f)). Before saving, the person MUST be shown a warning naming the pending revision, its status and its amount, and the PO MUST be saved only after their explicit confirmation; without confirmation nothing is saved. On confirmation, the PO's audit history MUST record, in the same save as the PO (FR-008): the confirming person, the time, the authoritative approved revision and its amount, the pending revision's number, status and amount at that moment, and that the warning was explicitly acknowledged. The PO MUST still reference the authoritative approved revision and match its amount exactly (FR-032). A later approval MUST NOT automatically modify that PO; it is then treated under FR-032b.
- **FR-032b**: When a newer revision is approved, every PO matched to an earlier approval MUST stay exactly as recorded and MUST be labelled "Matches superseded approval (revision n)"; it MUST NOT be cancelled, replaced or invalidated automatically, and an Administrator or Accountant cancels it under FR-031 when the client replaces it (OQ-35 a). A new PO MUST NOT reference a superseded approval (OQ-35 b). A Submitted PO referencing a superseded approval MUST NOT be verified, but MAY be rejected with a reason (OQ-35 c). Receipts linked to such a PO MUST still count in the outstanding balance, which is calculated against the authoritative approved client amount (FR-034; OQ-35 d). The project's current PO MUST be the current (Submitted or Verified) PO referencing the authoritative approval; if there is none, the project MUST show "No PO for the current approved amount" (OQ-35 e).
- **FR-033**: A client PO MUST NOT be treated as a vendor purchase or work order (PRD FR-10) or as an invoice.

#### Outstanding client balance (US6)

- **FR-034**: Each project MUST show its outstanding client balance as the authoritative approved client amount (FR-028) minus the sum of all verified, unreversed receipts, advances and subsequent payments alike (FR-019) (OQ-21, decided). It MUST be shown as soon as an approved revision exists; no purchase order is needed. It MUST state its basis (for example "Approved client amount ₹18,40,000.00 less verified receipts ₹6,50,000.00") and list the receipts counted; Submitted, Rejected, Withdrawn and Reversed receipts MUST NOT be counted. The approved client amount is that of the project's one quotation (FR-028; OQ-7).
- **FR-035**: When no basis exists, the balance MUST read "Not available" with the reason, never ₹0.00.
- **FR-036**: Before a receipt is saved as verified, or a Submitted receipt is verified, the system MUST check whether it would make the project's verified, unreversed receipts exceed the approved client amount. If so, it MUST show a warning with the approved amount, the current verified receipts, the new receipt amount and the resulting excess, and MUST save or verify only after explicit confirmation; without confirmation nothing changes. The confirmation MUST be recorded in the receipt's history, in the same save (FR-008), with the confirming person, the time and those four figures. When verified, unreversed receipts exceed the approved client amount, the balance MUST read "₹0.00 outstanding" with a separate "Overpaid by ₹X (incl. GST)"; a negative outstanding amount MUST never be shown. When no quotation revision is approved, no overpayment warning MUST be shown (FR-035) (OQ-22).
- **FR-037**: The only financial figures this feature shows are the approved client amount, the verified receipts total, and the outstanding balance or overpayment (FR-034, FR-036). No other derived metric, variance, margin, percentage, budget status, total across projects or forecast MUST be shown (OQ-31).

#### Client financial history and audit (US6)

- **FR-038**: Each project MUST show one client financial history listing every receipt, verification, rejection, reversal, quotation, revision, status change, approval and PO in date order, each with type, amount where it has one, state, person and time, and a link to the record (P-5, accepted). Existing advances and reversals MUST appear with their original dates and authors.
- **FR-039**: Every action MUST add a history entry with the action, the person, the time and previous and new values where they apply (PRD FR-15; Feature 003 FR-027). History MUST be readable by every role and MUST NOT be edited or deleted (Feature 003 FR-028). In the dashboard's Recent activity (Feature 005 FR-023), client financial entries added by this feature MUST show the event, project, actor, time and status, and MUST NOT expose any amount, on screen or in the response (OQ-28). The status is the associated record's state immediately after the event; every history entry this feature writes MUST capture it when written, for every action (creation, new revision, status change, approval, verification, rejection, withdrawal, reversal, cancellation, warning confirmation, attachment, removal and supersede), and existing history entries MUST NOT be rewritten. New Feature 006 events on Advance receipts show their status like any other. Feature 003 advance entries — an Advance recorded through the Feature 003 route, and the reversal of an Advance — MUST keep their exact existing response shape and presentation, with no status added. Every other client financial entry, including the reversal of a receipt that is not an Advance, is an entry added by this feature. Receipts that are not Advances MUST be shown as client receipts, separately from client advances (decisions 2026-11-18).
- **FR-040**: Existing client-advance history entries MUST stay attached to their advances and readable as today.

#### Financial documents (all stories)

- **FR-041**: Documents MUST use the existing Feature 003 documents (types, PDF/JPG/PNG/Word/Excel, 25 MB, content check, authenticated access, archive never delete, seven-year retention). A document MUST be attachable to a receipt, quotation revision, approval or PO of the same project (P-6, accepted 2026-11-17) under FR-001. Three document types MUST be added for project documents only: Quotation, Purchase order and Payment proof (OQ-25, OQ-42; see Backward Compatibility). Vendor documents MUST keep exactly the five Feature 003 types, and an upload of a vendor document with one of the new types MUST be refused.
- **FR-042**: Attaching MUST NOT copy or change the document; archiving a document MUST NOT detach it; attached documents MUST stay openable to every role. A Quotation document is required before a revision is approved (FR-026) and a Purchase order document before a PO is verified (FR-030); a Payment proof document is optional for receipts (OQ-25).
- **FR-042a**: Attaching a supporting document to a Draft revision (or any other record FR-001 allows) MUST be a separate attachment action, not an edit of the financial record: it MUST NOT change the record's amount, date, note or status, and it MUST add an entry to the record's audit history with the document, the person and the time. The history entry MUST also be reflected in the document's own history (FR-042c, F4). Attachment permissions by status are those of FR-001 and FR-042b. Removal and correction follow FR-042d and FR-042e.
- **FR-042b**: Who may attach a document depends on the status of the financial record (OQ-37, decided), for quotation revisions, receipts and purchase orders wherever the status exists:

  | Financial record status | Administrator | Accountant | Office Team |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Draft | Yes | Yes | Yes |
  | Submitted | Yes | Yes | Yes |
  | Under review | Yes | Yes | Yes |
  | Approved / Verified | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Rejected | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Withdrawn | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Reversed receipt | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Rejected receipt or purchase order | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Cancelled purchase order | Yes | Yes | No |
  | Client approval (with the approval or afterwards) | Yes | Yes | No |

  Attaching MUST be a separate auditable action and MUST NOT change the record's amount, date, note, status or approval (FR-042a). Every attachment MUST record the document, the actor and the actual attachment time; a document attached while a record is Under review keeps that time. Attaching MUST NOT reopen, or record any status change on, a Rejected, Withdrawn, Approved, Verified, Reversed or Cancelled record (OQ-40). Attachment permission gives no ownership of the record and no right to withdraw it (FR-025). Attaching to an approval, whether with the approval (FR-026) or afterwards, follows the "Client approval" row above.
- **FR-042c**: Feature 003 documents are reused under these existing rules, which this feature MUST NOT change (Feature 003 is not modified):
  - **F1 Archive**: every role may archive and restore a project document (Feature 003 FR-015), including one attached to a financial record. Archiving a document is not removing its attachment link: an archived attached document stays attached, marked archived, and openable (FR-042).
  - **F2 No replacement**: a corrected document MUST be uploaded as a new project document and attached; no action may overwrite a document or replace a document version (Feature 003 FR-029). A "supersedes" reference (FR-042e) is information on the new attachment, not a document version.
  - **F3 Archived projects**: no document may be uploaded, archived or restored on an archived project (Feature 003 FR-015), so no new file can be uploaded there for attachment. Attaching an existing document to, or removing an attachment from, a financial record of an archived project MUST also be refused (FR-010; OQ-26).
  - **F4 History on the record and the documents**: each attachment action and each removal (FR-042d) MUST be recorded in the financial record's history and in the document's history; a supersede (FR-042e) MUST also be recorded in the superseded document's history (OQ-38). Feature 003 FR-027 lists only upload, archive and restore for documents; these are added events on the document, not a change to them (see Backward Compatibility). **Planning constraint**: each attachment action and each removal MUST result in two history entries, one for the financial record and one for the document, and each attachment with a "supersedes" reference MUST result in three, adding one for the superseded document; all entries of one action are saved together (FR-008). Whether financial records become their own history targets or their entries are recorded on the project is a design choice left to `/speckit-plan`; this specification changes no database or implementation.
  - **F5 Document types**: the Feature 003 FR-012 type list is unchanged unless OQ-25 decides otherwise.
  - **F6 One document, several records**: decided (OQ-36 D7, D7b) — see FR-042f.
  - **F7 Archived documents**: decided (OQ-36 D8) — an archived document is not newly attached until it is restored; see FR-042f. Being viewable does not make it attachable.
  - **F8 Retention**: every document MUST remain retrievable for seven years (Feature 003 FR-016); removing an attachment (FR-042d) MUST NOT delete, archive or alter the document.
  - **F9 Same project only**: only documents belonging to the same project as the financial record may be attached (FR-041); vendor-owned documents MUST NOT be attached.
- **FR-042d**: An attachment link MAY be removed only as a recorded removal (OQ-36 D1), and only while the financial record is in an active status: a quotation revision in Draft, Submitted or Under review, or a receipt or PO that is Submitted (D2). Removal MUST be refused for every role on an Approved, Verified, Rejected, Withdrawn, Reversed or Cancelled record, and for every attachment on an approval, whether attached with the approval or afterwards (decision 2026-11-18). Removal MUST also be refused, for every role and in every status, for an attachment that supersedes another attachment or that has been superseded by another attachment (OQ-39); such attachments are corrected only by a further supersede (FR-042e). The roles that may remove are those FR-042b allows to attach in that status (D3), further limited so that an Office Team member may remove only attachments they added, checked against the recorded attacher, while Administrators and Accountants may remove any (D4). A reason MUST be given by every role; a request without one MUST be refused (D5). A removal MUST NOT erase the attachment record or delete, archive or change the document (FR-042c F8); it MUST be recorded in the financial record's history and the document's history, saved together (FR-008; FR-042c F4), with the document, person, time and reason, and MUST be refused if the record or attachment changed since it was loaded (FR-007). Each record MUST show a Removed Attachments list giving, for each removed attachment, the document, the person who removed it, the time and the reason; every removed document MUST stay openable to every role (D9).
- **FR-042e**: A corrected document MUST be uploaded as a new project document and attached under FR-042b (FR-042c F2). The new attachment MAY carry a "supersedes" reference to an earlier attachment (D6); when it does, a reason MUST be given by every role, and a request without one MUST be refused (OQ-38). The superseded attachment MUST stay attached and visible, labelled "Superseded by" the new document, and from then on neither the superseded nor the superseding attachment can be removed (FR-042d; OQ-39). Superseding MUST NOT change either document, the record or its status, and the "supersedes" reference and its reason MUST be recorded in the record's history, the new document's history and the superseded document's history, saved together (FR-042c F4; OQ-38). Because removal is not allowed on final records, superseding is the way to correct an attachment on an Approved, Verified, Rejected, Withdrawn or Reversed record. A "supersedes" reference MUST point to an attachment of the same financial record that is current (not removed) and not already superseded; any other reference MUST be refused (OQ-38). Each attachment can therefore be superseded at most once, and the superseding attachment may itself be superseded later, forming a chain. A supersede chain MUST stay permanently attached and auditable: no attachment in it may be removed, no label may change, and no earlier attachment state is reverted or restored automatically (OQ-39). A wrong correction is corrected by a new document and a new supersede relationship.
- **FR-042f**: One project document MAY be attached to several financial records of the same project (D7); each link is separate, removing one MUST NOT affect the others, and the document's history MUST list every link. The same document MUST NOT be attached twice to the same record at the same time; such a request MUST be refused (D7b). A document whose link to a record was removed MAY be attached to that record again under FR-042b; this MUST create a new attachment with its own person and time, and the earlier link, its removal and their history MUST remain unchanged (OQ-38). An archived document MUST NOT be newly attached: the request MUST be refused with a message that the document must be restored first (D8). Restoring follows Feature 003 FR-015, which refuses restoring on an archived project. Documents already attached when they are archived stay attached (FR-042c F1).

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Project** (existing): owns every client financial record.
- **Client receipt**: money received from the client — type, amount, date received, method, reference, note, documents, author, time, state (Submitted, Verified, Rejected, Withdrawn or Reversed). **Existing client advances (Feature 003) are client receipts of type Advance**, kept as they are (P-1). Never changed.
- **Receipt verification / rejection**: a reviewer's decision on a submitted receipt — outcome, reason (for rejection), person, time (P-2). New entries; the receipt is never edited.
- **Receipt reversal** (existing, Feature 003): full-amount correction of a verified receipt with a reason.
- **Client quotation**: the one quotation to the client on a project (OQ-7), with a system-generated reference (OQ-14) — title, author, time; one or more revisions, each with its own status, at most one of them active at a time (FR-023a); shows its authoritative approved revision (FR-028).
- **Quotation revision**: system-generated number, amount, quotation date, submission date (automatic), optional validity date, reason (from revision 2), note, documents, person, time, and its own current status (FR-025). Its details never change; its status changes only through FR-025.
- **Revision status change**: one allowed change (FR-025) of one revision, with previous and new status, reason (required for Rejected and Withdrawn), note, person, time.
- **Client approval**: the Under review → Approved change of one revision — revision approved, approved client amount (equal to that revision's amount), optional client acceptance date, note, documents (a Quotation document is required, FR-026), person, time. Never changed; the most recent is authoritative, earlier ones are shown as superseded (FR-027, FR-028).
- **Client purchase order**: client's PO number, PO date, amount (equal to the referenced approval's amount), the approved quotation it references, note, documents, author, time, state (Submitted, Verified, Rejected, Withdrawn or Cancelled) (OQ-3, OQ-4). Never changed; at most one Submitted or Verified PO per approved revision.
- **PO verification / rejection**: as for receipts.
- **PO cancellation**: a reviewer's cancellation of a Verified PO — reason, person, time (FR-031). A new entry; the PO is never edited.
- **Receipt or PO withdrawal**: the withdrawal of a Submitted receipt or PO by its Office Team creator or a reviewer — reason, person, time (FR-018, FR-030). A new entry; final; no resubmission.
- **Attachment link**: one link between a financial record and one project document — the record, the document, the person who attached it, the actual attachment time, an optional "supersedes" reference, with its reason, to one current attachment of the same record that is not already superseded (FR-042e), and, if removed, the person, time and reason of the removal (FR-042d). Never edited or erased; removal is a recorded entry and the link then appears in the Removed Attachments list. A link that supersedes or has been superseded by another link can never be removed (OQ-39). One document may have links to several records of the same project, but at most one current link per record; a document may be linked to a record again after its earlier link was removed, as a new link (FR-042f).
- **Document** (existing, Feature 003) and **history entry** (existing, Feature 003): reused unchanged; documents gain attachment, removal and supersede events in their history (FR-042c, F4).

- **C1 — PRD, Constitution I and VI (resolved 2026-11-17)**: client POs, subsequent receipts and outstanding client balances were not in the PRD. FR-19 is approved and placed in a new PRD Phase 4 — Client Financial Management (section 5.4); Phases 1 to 3 are unchanged (OQ-29).
- **C2 — Append-only (Constitution III)**: verification, rejection and approval must be new entries, never a change to the record (FR-005, P-2). Drafts are not edited in place either (OQ-9, decided).
- **C3 — Feature 003 FR-017 superseded**: the confirmed permissions let Office Team enter advances; FR-017 forbade it. Recorded in Backward Compatibility; reviewer entries keep today's behaviour.
- **C4 — Dashboard money tiles with unverified entries**: Feature 003 tiles sum every non-reversed advance. Resolved (OQ-27): they count only verified, unreversed advances, so Office Team submissions never change them before verification. Existing records are unaffected.
- **C5 — Document types (Feature 003 FR-012)**: resolved (OQ-25, OQ-42) — three types are added for project documents only, superseding FR-012 additively for them; vendor documents are unchanged (Backward Compatibility).
- **C6 — Q2 with Q3 and OQ-3 (resolved 2026-11-17 by OQ-8 and OQ-32)**: a changed amount is a new revision, starting in Draft with its own status; the Approved revision never changes. Purchase orders matched to an earlier approval follow FR-032b (OQ-35, decided).

Owner decision: Feature 004 excludes an in-app client approval workflow as a prerequisite for project creation; Feature 006's quotation approval is a separate financial workflow that establishes the authoritative approved client amount and does not control project creation (Clarifications, Session 2026-11-23).
- **C8 — Invoicing excluded, receivables included**: the financial reference (§19) keeps client invoicing out of scope; the outstanding balance rests on the approved client amount, not on invoices (FR-034; OQ-21, decided).

- **R2 — Amounts in Recent activity**: resolved (OQ-28) — client financial entries show no amounts (FR-039).
- **R3 — Naming**: the folder and branch keep `006-project-financial-foundation` while the feature is titled Client Financial Management; renaming the branch is not proposed.

Today each history entry has exactly one target, and documents have no attachment events, so the plan must provide two entries per attachment or removal, three per supersede, and new history actions; it chooses between new history target types for financial records and recording them on the project.
